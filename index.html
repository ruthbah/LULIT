A little piece of Ethiopia

Every piece at LULIT is handmade by artisans working in traditional Ethiopian techniques: hand woven cotton, hand forged beadwork, hand tooled leather and hand embroidered tibeb.

Nothing here is mass produced, so what you see today may not be here tomorrow.

The Collection

Traditional Clothing

Hand-woven habesha kemis, netela shawls and cultural dress, woven on traditional looms in cotton and silk-blend cloth with hand-embroidered tibeb borders.

Jewelry

Hand strung beaded necklaces made in traditional Ethiopian beadwork, with bold medallions, long fringe, and pieces built to be worn as a statement.

Accessories

Hand woven scarves, clutch pouches and small finishing touches, made using the same techniques passed down through generations of Ethiopian craftspeople.

Home Decor

Hand woven baskets and decorative pieces, made using traditional Ethiopian basket weaving.

Experience the Culture

The collection is best seen in person, so reach out to ask about a piece, current stock, or to arrange a visit.
